Health timeline

What happens when you quit

The same timeline the app follows for you, hour by hour.

20 minutes

Your heart rate and blood pressure drop.

12 hours

Carbon monoxide in your blood drops to normal.

2 days

Your sense of taste and smell start to improve.

3 days

Your bronchial tubes relax and breathing gets easier.

2 weeks

Circulation improves and lung function increases.

3 months

Coughing and shortness of breath decrease.

1 year

Your risk of coronary heart disease is half that of a smoker.

5 years

Your stroke risk approaches that of a non-smoker.

10 years

Your risk of dying from lung cancer is about halved.

Sources: World Health Organization (WHO), CDC. Tar and nicotine figures are average per-cigarette estimates. This information is general and not medical advice.

Questions

Is Exhale free?

Yes. The counter, the craving rescue, the health timeline and the first days of the program are free, with ads. Plus removes the ads and opens the full 30-day program; the yearly plan starts with a free week.

Does it work for vaping?

Yes. Vape mode counts puffs and pods instead of cigarettes and has its own health timeline.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and what you enter is stored only on your phone.

Is it medical advice?

No. Exhale is a support tool. For medical help, talk to your doctor or your country’s quitline.

How do I get in touch?

Email support@moonarcworks.com. Questions, ideas and bug reports are all welcome.
